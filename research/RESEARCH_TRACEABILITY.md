# Research traceability

Sentinal X 1.0.0 combines the existing satellite collection/detection pipeline with conservative evidence gating and a local validation workflow. It does **not** reproduce, train or ensemble all 20 papers. Sources below replace mismatched footnotes in the supplied table. Methods were checked against primary abstracts, papers or author repositories; full experimental reproductions were not performed. Limitations and engineering responses below are operational judgments, not quotations or claims that authors overlooked them.

## Bangladesh evaluation protocol

1. Obtain licensed satellite scenes spanning independent sites, seasons (including monsoon), cloud conditions and sensors. Preserve acquisition times, source IDs, footprint, native resolution and processing history. Do not replace native resolution with upsampled pixel spacing.
2. Independently annotate aircraft boxes over an explicitly defined fully reviewed footprint, including scenes with zero visible aircraft. Audit labels with another reviewer; document visibility and ambiguous labels. The evaluator does not automatically assess annotation quality.
3. Assign geographically separated spatial groups to train, calibration and test; keep all patches of a source scene within one split. The validator catches declared group/source leakage, but does not calculate geographic buffers or detect deceptive IDs.
4. Train/fine-tune outside this package on the training partition. Fix score thresholds with the calibration partition. Export predictions for the same frozen test scenes from baseline and each fusion ablation, including no-prediction scenes through the manifest.
5. Report AP at selected IoU, precision, recall, F1, FP/km², proposal Brier/ECE and country/sensor/season strata. Review low sample counts manually. Proposal calibration is conditional on generated detections; it is not probability of aircraft absence.
6. Evaluate repeated independent regions before deployment. This release has no measured Bangladesh accuracy, no uncertainty interval estimator, no OBB evaluator, no trained new fusion head, and no guarantee of anomaly attribution.

## Evidence interpretation

Optical/SAR candidates are screened using their own scores; thermal/foundation rows cannot rescue a weak detector. Scores remain heuristic and uncalibrated. Optional `validation_id` is an audit reference supplied by the operator, not proof of independent validation. Native resolution missing from metadata is explicitly unverified. CSV `native_gsd_m`, `enhancement`, `validation_id` extend the existing schema; legacy files remain accepted.

Movement uses reciprocal nearest candidates only between adjacent available acquisition epochs at a site, with an ambiguity margin, a default 72-hour gap cap and explicit registration uncertainty. This is a displacement screening heuristic, never identity association or a flight path. No observation/no detection remains unknown. Distinct detections from the same model are not collapsed by proximity; cross-model proximity fusion remains a heuristic and may require manual separation in crowded aprons.
