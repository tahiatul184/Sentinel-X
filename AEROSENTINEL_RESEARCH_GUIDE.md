# Research guide

Start with [START_HERE.txt](START_HERE.txt) and [SATELLITE_AWARENESS.md](SATELLITE_AWARENESS.md). Evaluate aircraft detectors on independently labelled scenes across optical and SAR sensors, aircraft sizes, seasons, cloud conditions, view angles, and sites. Calibrate scores and measure false positives and missed detections before operational interpretation. Review native image resolution, registration error, acquisition time, and alternate explanations for each candidate. Foundation embeddings and thermal surface diagnostics alone are not object detections.
