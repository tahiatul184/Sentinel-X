AEROSENTINEL SATELLITE IMAGE ARCHIVE
===================================

Successful Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, Landsat and multisatellite-fusion analytical
outputs are mirrored into this folder automatically.

After the first archived scene you will see:
  image_log.jsonl
  latest_sentinel-1.json
  latest_sentinel-2.json
  latest_landsat-8-9.json
  latest_fusion.json

If no imagery appears, first confirm that an AOI/location has been saved, then
check System Health and app\data\collection\logs\multisatellite.log.
